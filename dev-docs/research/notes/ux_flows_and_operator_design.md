Research date: 2026-10-23. Method: web searches only; no primary papers read, no apps run. Tiers: **[doc]** vendor or official documentation, **[forum]** user reports, **[secondary]** a summary of a paper by a third party, **[inferred]** our application of the above. ## 2. Human-factors research behind the operator-screen question

- **Situation awareness (Endsley 1995):** three levels, perception, comprehension, projection. A cited analysis reports 76.3% of SA errors were perception errors, so the display should make the state perceivable at a glance; some studies also report that more information can create overconfidence ("less is more"). [secondary] [overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation_awareness), [NASA](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20020030334/downloads/20020030334.pdf), [dashboards review](https://www.perceptualedge.com/blog/?p=45)
- **High-performance HMI (ISA-101):** neutral gray background, saturated colour reserved for abnormal conditions, four-level display hierarchy from overview down to diagnostics; claimed benefits are better abnormal-situation detection and shorter training. Vendor hex values are not part of the standard. [secondary] [ISA blog](https://blog.isa.org/the-high-performance-hmi), [Tatsoft](https://docs.tatsoft.com/display/FX/ISA-101+HMI+Compliance+How-to), [IPD white paper](https://resources.ipd.com.au/documents/white-papers/Best-practices-for-high-performance-HMI-design_White%20Paper_ENUS_2023.pdf)
- **Mode error (Sarter and Woods 1995; Norman):** adding modes without support for tracking them creates new error types; a 1999 survey found 73% of 1,268 pilots had inadvertently selected a wrong mode. Implication: a design/show split is only safe if the current mode is unmistakable and there are few modes. [secondary] [SWOV record](https://swov.nl/en/publicatie/how-world-did-we-ever-get-mode-mode-error-and-awareness-supervisory-control), [NASA mode confusion](https://shemesh.larc.nasa.gov/fm/papers/ModeConfusionAnalysisUsingFormalMethods.pdf)
- **Legibility:** AVIXA/ANSI DISCAS sets maximum viewing distance as a multiple of image height by task (about 8, 6, 4); the "text height x 150 = max distance" rule is a vendor heuristic, not the standard. [secondary] [AVIXA DISCAS](https://api.avixa.org/standards/discas-calculators/discas/learn-more-about-display-size), [Epson white paper](https://www.epson.co.nz/sizematters/education/download/Display%20Size%20Whitepaper%20-%20Education%20-%20NZ.pdf)
- **Church slide guidance (practitioner, no controlled study found):** about 20-40 words and at most 4 lines per slide, sans-serif at 24 pt or larger (40 better), test from the back of the room; sources disagree on dark versus light backgrounds. Our sample deck runs denser (median 88 words, 9 lines, 25 pt) because it carries continuous responsive liturgy, so these are guides to measure against, not rules. [secondary] [MediaShout](https://mediashout.com/readable-church-presentations/?amp=1), [accessibility guide](https://www.anabaptistdisabilitiesnetwork.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Best-practices-for-the-big-screen-in-worship.pdf), [Church Motion Graphics](https://www.churchmotiongraphics.com/blog/3-effective-scripture-slide-designs/)
- **Multimedia learning (Mayer):** signaling helps (cues such as bold), redundancy hurts (the same words on several channels), segmenting supports pacing. Only practitioner summaries were retrieved, not Mayer's papers. [secondary] [summary](https://www.nu.edu/blog/multimedia-design-principles)

## 3. Implications for StreamDeck [inferred]

1. Keep the plan-first model (service order as source), matching Planning Center/Proclaim.
2. Two modes only (Design, Show), a persistent mode banner, structural edits locked in Show, and a narrow in-place text fix for live corrections.
3. Operator screen is the overview level: Program, Next, a one-column rundown with the current item, big Prev/Next and a Clear-to-focus-screen key, a few scene buttons, one health chip that is neutral until something is wrong. Cameras, audio and network are one click down.
4. Program is the single source of truth; nothing in Design affects live without an explicit Take (OBS leak reports show what goes wrong otherwise).
5. A pre-service check screen separate from both modes, and a confidence/stage display for people reading from the front.
6. Avoid showing the same text on two stream channels at once (redundancy).
7. Validate with the real operator: a rehearsal with a clickable prototype, since the evidence above is mostly secondary.
